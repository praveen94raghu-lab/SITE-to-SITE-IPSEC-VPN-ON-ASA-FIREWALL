SITE TO SITE IPSEC VPN CONFIGURATION
HQ:

crypto ikev1 policy 10
hash sha
authentication pre-share
group 2
lifetime 86400
encryption 3des
exit

tunnel-group 100.50.10.6 type ipsec-l2l
tunnel-group 100.50.10.6 IPsec-attributes
ikev1 pre-shared-key cisco
exit

crypto IPsec ikev1 transformer-set TSET esp-3des esp-sha-hmac

access-list VPN-ACL permit ip 192.168.10.0 255.255.255.0 192.168.20.0 255.255.255.0

crypto map CMAP 10 set peer 100.50.10.6
crypto map CMAP 10 set ikev1 transformer-set TSET
crypto map CMAP 10 match address VPN-ACL
crypto map CMAP interface OUTSIDE
crypto ikev1 enable OUTSIDE

wr mem
